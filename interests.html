05 / Life beyond the screen

Away from the screen.
Still exploring.

Technology is a major part of my life, but curiosity carries into the rest of my interests too. Whether I’m out on the trail, working in the garden, experimenting with my setup, or cooking outdoors, I enjoy learning through hands-on experiences.

Hiking & Backpacking ↘Gardening & Growing ↘Technology & Tinkering ↘Cooking & Outdoor Living ↘

01 / Find a different pace

Hiking & Backpacking

I enjoy getting outdoors, especially hiking and backpacking in places like Red River Gorge. Trips like these give me time to explore, challenge myself, and enjoy the kind of hands-on experience that I value in other parts of life too.

02 / Watch things grow

Gardening & Growing

Gardening has become one of the ways I enjoy learning through experimentation. From planning raised beds to growing vegetables, herbs, and garlic, I like seeing how small decisions affect progress over time.

03 / Follow the question

Technology & Tinkering

Outside of school projects, I enjoy learning more about computers, hardware, and home technology. Exploring setups, tools, and practical experiments helps me stay curious and keeps technology interesting beyond the classroom.

04 / Make something good

Cooking & Outdoor Living

I also enjoy cooking and trying different meals, whether that’s at home or outdoors. It’s another interest that connects back to experimenting, improving over time, and enjoying practical results.

Different interests. Shared curiosity.

More than one side
of the story.

These interests shape how I think, learn, and approach new challenges. They’re a reminder that curiosity doesn’t stop with school or technical projects.
