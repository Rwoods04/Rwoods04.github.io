I'm Robert Woods, a Computer Information Technology student at Western Kentucky University with a growing interest in software development, networking, and understanding how technology works behind the scenes.

I earned my Associate in Applied Science in Computer and Information Technologies, with a concentration in Programming, from Elizabethtown Community and Technical College.

I enjoy learning how systems work, experimenting with new tools, and building projects that help me develop practical technical skills.
